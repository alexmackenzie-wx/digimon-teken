How to Play

The goal

Hits raise your opponent's damage %. The higher it is, the further they fly. Knock them past the edge of the screen to take a stock. Last Digimon standing wins!

Digivolve!

Dealing and taking damage fills your DIGI meter. When it flashes, press Digivolve to evolve into your Champion form for 15 seconds: bigger, stronger, with brand new special moves.

Agumon → Greymon · Gabumon → Garurumon · Patamon → Angemon · Guilmon → Growlmon

Items

🍖 Digi-Meat heals 25%. 💠 Data Chips fill your DIGI meter (or extend your Champion time).
